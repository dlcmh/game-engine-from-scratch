# Governance

## Ownership

This is a personal project owned by **David Chin** (@dlcmh), who is the sole maintainer and final decision-maker on direction, architecture, and what gets merged.

## Delegated authority

AI coding agents are granted full standing authorship rights in this repository:

- Agents may autonomously **commit and push to `main`** at their own discretion.
- This includes documentation, configuration, and ordinary code changes.
- Agents must not force-push, rewrite history, or delete branches/files they did not create without explicit human approval.
- Every autonomous action must be reported faithfully in the session summary (what was done, what failed, what was skipped).

## Changes to this document

The maintainer may amend this document at any time. Agents should flag (not silently apply) any conflict they notice between this document and actual instructions given in a session — direct session instructions from the maintainer take precedence.
