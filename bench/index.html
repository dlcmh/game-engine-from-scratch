Fan speed and temperature are not exposed by macOS userland APIs or iOS Safari, so thermal stress is measured by its observable effect: FPS decay under sustained load. On a Mac you can cross-check with iStat Menus or sudo powermetrics. Keep this tab in the foreground for the whole run — iOS suspends rendering in background tabs.
